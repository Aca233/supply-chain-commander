## Design Context

Supply Chain Commander is a dense economic simulation game about factories,
goods, wages, production methods, and market pressure. Its production UI should
feel like an industrial operations dashboard, not a landing page or decorative
showcase.

Preferred interface direction:
- Dark graphite and muted steel surfaces with thin dividers, compact rows, and
  strong numeric hierarchy.
- Profit, revenue, costs, input/output flow, bottlenecks, and production methods
  are primary signals.
- Labor and wages are important operating costs, but should stay compact unless
  the player is actively adjusting them.
- Avoid emoji-heavy controls, oversized glowing cards, nested cards, and
  one-note neon/purple/blue styling.
- Use existing project icons and design-system controls. Do not add a new icon
  dependency just for this surface.
- Build for repeat use: scanning, comparing, and changing a building quickly
  matter more than dramatic presentation.
